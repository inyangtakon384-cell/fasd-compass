FASD Clinical Toolkit

Checklists, templates and learning for clinical teams, based on SIGN 156 and NICE QS204.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/clinical-toolkit/
Practical resources for paediatricians, GPs, nurses, health visitors, CAMHS and allied health professionals. They are based on SIGN 156 and NICE QS204 and are free to use and adapt.
🩺
An educational resource, not a diagnostic tool
These resources support clinical reasoning. They do not score, diagnose or rule out FASD, and they do not decide who should be referred. Diagnosis needs a multidisciplinary assessment using the SIGN 156 criteria.
