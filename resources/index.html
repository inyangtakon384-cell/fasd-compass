Evidence-Based Sources

All content in FASD Compass is based on peer-reviewed literature and current diagnostic guidelines.

Key Guidelines

📄Primary UK Guidelines
• SIGN 156 — Scottish Intercollegiate Guidelines Network. Children and Young People Exposed Prenatally to Alcohol. Edinburgh: SIGN; 2019 (revalidated 2022). SIGN publication no. 156. ISBN 978-1-909103-67-2.

• NICE QS204 — National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder. Quality Standard QS204. Published March 2022. Applies in England & Wales.

• SNOMED CT codes (implemented 2024): FASD with SFF (1894471000000108), FASD without SFF (1894461000000101), At increased risk (2078801000000102).

• DHSC Health Needs Assessment for FASD in England. September 2021.
📚Educational & Behavioural
• Pei, J. et al. (2016). Supporting students with FASD. Canadian Journal of Special Education.

• Blackburn, C. & Whitehurst, T. (2010). FASD: Emerging issues in awareness, prevention and intervention.

• Streissguth, A.P. & Kanter, J. (1997). The challenge of FASD in the 21st century.
