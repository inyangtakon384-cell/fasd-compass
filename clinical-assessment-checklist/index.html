FASD Clinical Assessment Checklist

A structured checklist for FASD assessment, following SIGN 156.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/clinical-assessment-checklist/
⬇️ Download PDF
A checklist for paediatricians, nurses and allied health professionals preparing for or carrying out an FASD assessment. It follows the structure of SIGN 156 and does not replace it. Tick boxes on screen are for your own use and are not saved.
🩺
An educational resource, not a diagnostic tool
These resources support clinical reasoning. They do not score, diagnose or rule out FASD, and they do not decide who should be referred. Diagnosis needs a multidisciplinary assessment using the SIGN 156 criteria.
📁Before the assessment
👶1. Developmental history
🍷2. Prenatal alcohol exposure, where available
📏3. Growth and physical examination
🧠4. Neurodevelopmental assessment domains
🏠5. Functional difficulties
🧩6. Co-occurring conditions
🔬7. Differential diagnoses
👥8. Multidisciplinary assessment
🗺️9. Referral and management planning
