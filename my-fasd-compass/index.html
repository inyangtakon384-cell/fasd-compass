My FASD Compass

Practical tools to plan support, build routines and manage everyday challenges at home.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/my-fasd-compass/
Practical tools for parents and carers, in one place. Make a support plan, build a picture routine, plan for big feelings and find ideas for everyday challenges. Every tool is free and works in your browser. Each one gives general ideas and is not an individual clinical assessment of your child.

🔒 Your information stays on this device. Nothing you type is sent to FASD Compass or anyone else. Tools with a Save button keep your work in this browser only, and you can clear it at any time.

Personalised Support Plan

Choose your child’s strengths and needs, and get an editable plan with strategies, changes to the environment, communication tips, school support and review goals. Download it as a PDF or Word document.

Visual Routine Builder

Make a picture routine for mornings, after school, homework, bedtime, appointments or changing activity. Choose the steps and pictures, change the order and print.

Emotional Regulation Toolkit
