Accessibility Statement

How we make FASD Compass usable for as many people as possible.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/accessibility/

Our aim

We want FASD Compass to be easy to use for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or voice control, and people who find reading hard.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Zoom up to 200% without the text spilling off the screen.
  • Use the site with a keyboard alone.
  • Use a screen reader to read headings, links and buttons.
  • Print most pages, or save them as PDF from your browser.

Known issues

We test the site with automated checks and by hand. These are the issues we know about:

  • Some downloadable PDFs have not yet been fully checked for screen reader use. If you need a document in another format, email us and we will help.
  • Some decorative icons are emoji, which a few screen readers may read aloud.
  • Some older pages may still have small colour contrast issues. We are fixing these as we find them.

Tell us about a problem

If something does not work for you, email eim@drtakon.com. Tell us the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 10 working days.

How we test

We use the axe-core automated checker on every page, check pages at mobile and desktop widths, and test keyboard use. This statement was prepared in November 2026.

Sources: WCAG 2.2: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

Last reviewed: November 2026 by Dr Inyang Takon, Consultant Neurodevelopmental Paediatrician.
