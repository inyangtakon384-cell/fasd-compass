While You Wait

Your child does not need a diagnosis to get support. Here is what you can ask for now.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/while-you-wait/
⬇️ Download PDF
Waiting lists for FASD assessment can be long. The good news is that your child does not need a diagnosis to get support. In England, neither the legal definition of special educational needs nor the definition of disability requires a diagnosis (IPSEA). Support should be based on what your child needs.

At School or Nursery

🏫SEN support
📄Education, Health and Care (EHC) needs assessment
If SEN support is not enough, you can ask the local authority for an EHC needs assessment yourself, at any time. You do not need the school's agreement or a diagnosis.
  • The local authority must reply within 6 weeks
  • If it agrees to assess, a final EHC plan must be issued within 20 weeks of your request
  • IPSEA has a free template letter to help you ask
🤝Free, impartial advice
Every area in England has a SENDIASS (special educational needs and disabilities information, advice and support service). It is free, and you do not need a diagnosis to use it. Find your local service.

In Scotland, Enquire advises on additional support for learning. In Wales, SNAP Cymru advises on additional learning needs.

Money and Practical Help

💷Disability Living Allowance (DLA)
DLA for children is for children under 16 who need much more looking after than other children of the same age, or who have difficulty getting about. The needs must have lasted at least 3 months and be expected to last at least 6 months. It is based on your child's needs, not a diagnosis. Young people aged 16 and over apply for Personal Independence Payment (PIP) instead.
🏠A parent carer needs assessment
You can ask your council's children with disabilities team for an assessment of your own needs as a carer. It looks at your wellbeing, work and family life, and can lead to support such as short breaks. Find your local council. Your council's Local Offer website also lists local SEND services and support groups.

At Home

💡Strategies that help now
FASD-informed approaches help many children, with or without a diagnosis. See the FASD Compass parents page and National FASD parenting tips.
🗂️Keep the referral moving

Support for You

📞Helplines
  • National FASD: 0208 458 5951
  • Contact, for families with disabled children: freephone 0808 808 3555. You do not need a diagnosis.
  • Carers UK: 0808 808 7777
👨‍👩‍👧Other families
Talking to families who understand can make a real difference. The FASD UK Alliance links local support groups across the UK. Find local support.
🚨
If you are worried about safety right now
Call NHS 111 for urgent advice, or 999 in an emergency.
