Personalised Support Plan

Create a support plan for your child in about ten minutes, then edit, print and share it.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/support-plan/
Answer a few simple questions about your child. FASD Compass will turn your answers into a support plan you can edit, print and share with school, clubs, relatives or professionals. Every child with FASD is different, so tick only what fits your child. You can change any wording before you print.

Using Your Plan

🏫Sharing with school
Ask for a meeting with the class teacher or SENCO and go through the plan together. Ask the school to share it with every adult who works with your child, including lunchtime staff and supply teachers. The School Advocacy Toolkit has more help.
🔁Keep it up to date
Review the plan every term, or sooner if something changes, such as a new class, a move or a change in medication. Children with FASD can have uneven development, so needs change over time.
📚 Several suggestions draw on National FASD parenting tips, including allowing extra processing time and believing a child who says they do not know.
