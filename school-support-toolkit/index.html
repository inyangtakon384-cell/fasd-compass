FASD School Support Toolkit

Free, practical tools to plan, deliver and review support for pupils with FASD or similar needs.
FASD Compass · fasdcompass.co.uk/school-support-toolkit/
Practical, free tools for teachers, SENCOs, ALNCos and support staff working with pupils who have FASD, possible FASD or similar needs. Everything here is needs-led. You do not need a diagnosis to use it.
🧭
Needs first, not labels
These tools do not diagnose or rule out FASD or any other condition. It is not a clinical assessment. Support in school should be based on the pupil’s needs and does not depend on a diagnosis.
